CALCULUS

The mathematical study of change, accumulation and continuous processes.

THE IDEA

Calculus is not merely a collection of formulas. It is a mathematical framework for understanding quantities that change and quantities that accumulate.

The central ideas of calculus emerge from a small number of deep problems: the tangent problem, the area problem and the study of infinite processes.
